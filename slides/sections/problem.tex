\section{Problem and datasets}
\begin{frame}{The problem}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Network $G=(V,E)$: junctions with coordinates, two sources, pipes with a capacity
    \item Pipe length $\ell(u,v)=\lVert p_u-p_v\rVert$; network distance $d_G$ under $\ell$
    \item Seven questions, one study: each answer feeds the next
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{0.4em}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
    T1 & how to store and explore the network \\
    T2 & how long the pipes are, how indirect the network is \\
    T3 & which source supplies each node, which pipes to close \\
    T4 & which node of each sector gets the oldest water \\
    T5 & how much water reaches that node, and what limits it \\
    T6 & shortest closed sampling walk from the office \\
    T7 & where a new node connects \\
  \end{tabular}
  \note{One graph model, seven questions from the challenge slides. With the instructor's approval I covered all seven, because they form a chain: lengths give distances, distances give sectors, sectors give the flow problems.}
\end{frame}
